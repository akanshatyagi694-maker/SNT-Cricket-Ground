About SNT Cricket Ground

Welcome to Shraddhanand Tyagi (SNT) Cricket Ground & Sports Academy, one of the finest cricket grounds in Ghaziabad. We provide a premium cricketing experience with a well-maintained ground for practice sessions, friendly matches, tournaments, and coaching. Located at Pipe Line Road near Bahadurpur Police Chowki, our ground offers a peaceful environment, quality playing conditions, and excellent facilities for players of all levels. Whether you are planning a weekend match, a tournament, or regular practice, SNT Cricket Ground is the perfect destination for your team.
